OUR STORY

From Local Fields to Your Front Door

Founded in 2020, AGRO-UP started with a simple observation: the journey from farm to table was too long, too processed, and too disconnected. We decided to change that.

We partner directly with certified organic farmers who prioritize soil health and biodiversity. By removing the middleman, we ensure that the spinach in your salad was likely in the ground just 24 hours ago.
